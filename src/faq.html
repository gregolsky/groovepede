Frequently asked questions

Which services work?

Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, Deezer, Amazon Music, Pandora, and SoundCloud. Paste any album link and Groovepede resolves cross-service links automatically via Odesli.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. No subscription, no ads, no upsell.

Do I need to log in?

No. Groovepede works without any account. Connecting Spotify is optional — it lets you sync your queue to a private Spotify playlist so it’s backed up and accessible across devices.

Where is my queue stored?

Entirely in your browser’s localStorage. Nothing leaves your device. You can export a backup JSON from the profile at any time.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Add it to your home screen (Safari on iOS or Chrome on Android) for a native-like experience. Android supports sharing albums directly from your music app; on iPhone you can paste links or use the share sheet.
